# Code of Conduct

ForgeGuardian follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) v2.1.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

**Positive behavior:**
- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Using signatures to target specific individuals or organizations without technical evidence
